Capabilities

Built for the moments that cannot wait

Everything below works from one app, one directory, and one audit trail.

Role-based routing

Message "on-call cardiology" instead of hunting for a name. Helix resolves the role to the clinician actually working that shift.

Escalation workflows

If a critical message is not acknowledged within the window you define, it moves up the chain automatically and records why.

Priority alerts

Code and emergency messages break through silent mode with a distinct tone, so urgency is never ambiguous.

Facility broadcasts

Reach a unit, a site, or the whole hospital at once — with delivery and read visibility for incident review.

Care team threads

Conversations grouped around a patient or a case, so context travels with the handover instead of living in someone's inbox.

Voice and video escalation

Move from text to a call in one tap when typing is slower than talking — without leaving the clinical thread.

How it works

From signal to action in three steps

1

Send to a role

A nurse messages "on-call paediatrics" from the ward. No directory lookup, no guessing who is covering tonight.

2

Helix resolves and delivers

The platform checks the live roster, delivers to the clinician on duty, and marks the message delivered and read.

3

Escalate if silence

No acknowledgement in the agreed window? The message escalates to the next tier and the whole path is recorded.

Why teams stop using group chat

  • Consumer apps have no on-call awareness — Helix does
  • Patient information stays inside a governed, auditable system
  • Staff who leave lose access immediately, not eventually
  • Read receipts are tied to a verified clinical identity
  • Incident reviews can reconstruct exactly what was sent, and when
