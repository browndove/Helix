Capabilities

Scheduling that respects how hospitals actually work

Grades, skills, leave, and fatigue rules — not just names in boxes.

Roster builder

Build by unit, team, and grade with repeating patterns. Copy last month forward and adjust only what changed.

Shift swaps with approval

Staff propose swaps in the app. Unit leads approve or decline, and every decision is logged with a timestamp.

Coverage alerts

Unfilled slots surface early and notify eligible staff, so gaps get closed before they turn into an unsafe shift.

Leave and absence

Annual leave, study leave, and sick cover are visible in the same view, so the roster reflects who is genuinely available.

On-call integration

The published roster drives message routing. Change the schedule and the on-call destination changes with it.

Personal shift view

Every clinician sees their own month, gets reminders before a shift, and can export it to a personal calendar.

The workflow

Plan once, publish everywhere

1

Draft the month

Start from last month's pattern, apply leave, and let Helix flag rule breaches while you work.

2

Publish to staff

One action pushes the roster to every phone and updates on-call routing at the same moment.

3

Manage the exceptions

Swaps, sick calls, and last-minute cover run through an approval trail instead of a phone call nobody recorded.

What this replaces

  • Spreadsheets emailed around with three versions in circulation
  • Printed rotas on a notice board that went out of date on Tuesday
  • Swap agreements made verbally and forgotten by the weekend
  • Calling four people to find out who is actually covering tonight
  • Month-end reporting rebuilt by hand from paper records
